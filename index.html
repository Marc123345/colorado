    How to read it
    Idaho is six markets, not one. About 2.03 million people, separated by mountains. Population is the headline, but commercial roof count is the real asset.
    Tier 1 is the campaign. About 930,000 people, all within 90 minutes of Boise, at the standard $1,500 plus $50 a day.
    Tier 2 is the best second market. Two hours down I-84, and almost all of the roof stock is low-slope metal. It makes sense for a contractor who will travel for a 40,000 square foot roof.
    Tier 3 is worth a pin, not a campaign. Few people, but the highest property values in Idaho.
    Tiers 4 to 6 are different businesses. North Idaho is seven hours by road through Oregon and Washington and works as part of the Spokane metro. Reaching it, or the east, takes its own crew.
    Ring distances on the map are straight-line. Mileage in the lists is by road from Boise. Commercial markers, Boise Foothills, Warm Springs Mesa and every money neighborhood outside Tier 1 are approximate. The tiers add up to about 1.98 million; grey counties sit outside them.
