How to read it
Radius: every pin sits inside a 30-mile circle on downtown Denver. That covers the Denver metro, about 3 million people, and Boulder County, about 330,000.

Boulder: modern flat-roof homes and owners who pay for energy efficiency, which is how you sell a reflective coating on a house.
Distances are straight-line from Thornton city center, not from the shop address. Morrison and Superior positions are approximate.
