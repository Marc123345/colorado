    How to read it
    Biggest number: run all three zones as one campaign. About 1,230,000 people.
    Most efficient: start with Zone A at the standard $1,500 plus $50 a day. It is one contiguous metro, and a 30-mile radius on Boise covers it. Zone C is the upsell once the first month works.
    Roofs matter more than people. Population is the headline, but commercial roof count is the real asset, and that sits in Zones A and C.
    Zone C is two hours each way. It only makes sense for a contractor who will travel for commercial jobs. Many coaters will for a 40,000 square foot roof.
    Ring distances on the map are straight-line. Mileage in the lists is by road from Boise. Commercial markers, Boise Foothills and Warm Springs Mesa are approximate.
